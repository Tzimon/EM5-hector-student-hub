# Function 3 – Specification: Structured Feedback & Improvement Plan (HTML Mockup)

---

## 1. Idea & Goal

- Complement the existing **academic grading system** with **structured, personalized and actionable feedback**.
- Purpose: support students' **professional and personal development** throughout their master's studies.

---

## 2. Main Functionalities

### 2.1 Receive Feedback

- **When:** after relevant presentations, projects, assignments or evaluations.
- **What is assessed:**
  - academic performance, **and**
  - specific professional competencies, e.g.:
    - communication
    - presentation skills
    - critical thinking
    - problem-solving
    - teamwork
    - leadership
    - etc.
- **Each assessment includes:**
  - clear evaluation criteria
  - identified strengths
  - areas for improvement
  - concrete examples explaining the feedback
- **Who gives feedback** (depending on the activity):
  - lecturers
  - peers
  - potentially industry mentors
- **Feedback content (per feedback giver):**
  1. What the student did well.
  2. What could be improved, supported by a concrete example.
  3. One practical recommendation for the next activity.
- **Access:** via a **"View Feedback"** option **next to the grade**.

### 2.2 Improvement Plan

- Students can **select a specific recommendation** and create a simple **improvement action**, including:
  - an improvement goal
  - a concrete next step
  - an optional support person (lecturer, peer, or mentor)
  - a follow-up opportunity to review progress
- **Value:** this transforms feedback from something students read once into something they can act on.
